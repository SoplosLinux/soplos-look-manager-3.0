# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [3.0.0-beta] - 2026-11-12

First beta: the pages of `3.0.0-1-alpha` were tried on a Plasma desktop. The last two pages of the Windows group, Behavior and Rules, are new in this version and have not had that use yet. Everything done so far is listed under `3.0.0-1-alpha` below.

## [3.0.0-1-alpha] - 2026-11-11

### Fixed
- Choosing a wallpaper did not change the desktop. `plasma-apply-wallpaperimage` says that it has set it and saves the new picture, but Plasma goes on showing the old one. The wallpaper is set now as the layout scripts of the themes of Soplos do: the picture goes in `Image` and in `PreviewImage` of every desktop and the configuration is loaded again.
- Wallpapers are set as soon as the card is clicked, so the page has no Apply button.
- Application style did nothing when applied: the programs that were open were not told that the style changed. They are told now, as System Settings does.
- Window decorations: every Aurorae theme was applied as an SVG one, so a QML theme left the windows with no decorations. The kind is read from the files of the theme (`main.qml` or `decoration.svg`), and a folder that is neither is not offered.
- Application style, Plasma style and Window decorations showed no pictures. Plasma draws them with code of its own, straight on its window, and does not keep them as files, so a small helper, `soplos-look-manager-style-preview`, draws them with the same libraries and the same calls as those pages of System Settings: the controls of the style with `QStyleFactory` and the color scheme in use (`KColorScheme`), the frame and the analog clock of the Plasma theme with `KSvg`, and the decoration loaded from its plugin of KWin (`KDecoration3`). Each picture is drawn once and kept in `~/.cache/soplos-look-manager/thumbs`; it is drawn again when the style, the theme or the color scheme changes.
- The pictures fill the card, and are laid out as the pages of System Settings lay them out: the frame and the clock of the Plasma theme fill the picture, the controls of the style are drawn as big as the card allows, and a window decoration is shown as two windows, an inactive one behind and the active one in front of it. All of them are drawn on the colors of the color scheme in use.
- The color schemes are drawn as the Colors page of System Settings draws them (`kcms/colors/ui/main.qml`): a window with its title bar, a label and a button, and a frame with normal, highlighted and disabled text, each with a link and a visited link. The cards of Colors, Application style, Plasma style and Window decorations are wide (16:9), as the ones of the themes and as the cards of System Settings are, and the pictures drawn for them have that shape.
- Application style is drawn with the widgets of `kcms/style/stylepreview.ui`: a tab widget with a check box, two radio buttons, a combo box, a push button, a spin box, a slider, a progress bar and a scroll bar.
- The hands of the clock of Plasma style were out of place: the hour hand turned on a point that was not the center. They are drawn now as `ThemePreview.qml` and `Hand.qml` draw them: at the natural size of the element times the scale of the face, turning on the rotation center of the theme or on the middle of the element, with no shadows.
- Plastik, and the other window decorations written in QML (Aurorae), showed no picture. Aurorae makes a renderer of its own, which needs OpenGL, unless the decoration is given an item to draw in (a `visualParent`), as the page of System Settings does. They are drawn now in a Qt Quick scene with the software renderer, each window on its own and the two together as on the page. The title of the windows is the name that the list shows. One that cannot be drawn keeps its icon.
- The pictures of Application style, Plasma style and Window decorations follow the light or the dark theme of the program, as the rest of the window does, and are drawn again when it changes. They are drawn with the Breeze Light or the Breeze Dark color scheme; the colors of the Plasma themes that bring their own stay theirs.
- The pictures drawn by the helper were in English while the rest of the cards were in the language of the program. The texts of the controls of Application style are in the language of the program now (the 8 of it), and the pictures are drawn again when the language changes. The picture of Application style has almost no margin around its controls, and the window of the cards of Colors is drawn with a margin, a border and a shadow, as a window on the card and not stuck to its edges.
- The cards of Colors have the shape of the cards of the themes (16:9) and not a taller one.
- The hands of the clock of Plasma style turned on the middle of the element. `Hand.qml` puts the rotation center, when the theme has no hint for it, at half of the width on both axes, where the hub of the hand is, so the minute hand was drawn off its hub. It is drawn that way now.
- The window decorations are drawn with their shadow, as the page does, so the windows stand out from the background, with the name of the decoration as the title of the windows and the icon of the menu button taken from the icon theme in use.
- The pictures of the icon themes and of the cursor themes are kept in the cache and made again only when the theme, or one that it inherits from, changes: the six icons of a theme were looked for in its folders every time, and the cursors were kept for ever, even when the theme was updated.
- Panel: a new panel could not be filled, so it was of no use. The page lists the elements of the panel (the application launcher, the tasks, the system tray, the clock...) in their order, adds any widget of Plasma to it (a search over the ones installed, with their names in the language of the program) and takes them off. The widgets are listed, added and removed through the scripting API of Plasma (`widgetIds`, `addWidget` and `remove`, from `shell/scripting` of plasma-workspace 6.7.4). That API cannot move a widget (`index` does nothing), so a new element goes at the end of the panel, and the page says it. The elements are put on the panel at once, with no need to press Apply.
- Panel: the page has the drawing of the panel on its own at the top, and under it four cards in two columns of the same height each (1 Elements, 2 Position and size with the styles, the position, the thickness and floating, 3 Length and alignment, 4 Look and behavior), and no paragraphs of explanation (what the drawing is, how the order of the elements works and where Apply acts are tooltips now), instead of five cards of settings piled in one column next to a small drawing. It was laid out and checked at the size of the window of the program (1000 by 700, 860 by 560 at the least).
- Panel: the drawing of the screen is not deformed to fit the cards. It has the proportions of the screen that the window is on, which the window itself knows (`window.screen`, in the same logical pixels as the panels), the thickness of the panel is drawn as it is and not thicker, and the drawing takes the whole width of the page, with no space at the sides, and it follows the light or the dark theme of the program (the wallpaper, the window, the icons and the panel are drawn light or dark). It shows the elements that are on the panel, each with its icon.
- The pages that list things (themes, colors, application style, Plasma style, window decorations, icons, cursors, GTK, sounds, start-up, wallpapers and login) have no Apply button, no line with the number of items and the one in use, and no explanatory text any more: a click on a card applies it (a global theme asks first what to apply, and the login screen asks for the password). Apply stays on the Panel page only, where several settings are changed together. The cards have no tooltip with the description of the theme.
- The banner of every page stays where it is while the page scrolls under it.
- Login: the NumLock switch is gone. It could not tell the state of the greeter: its file (`/var/lib/plasmalogin/.config/kcminputrc`) is in a folder that only `plasmalogin` and root can read (0750), so the switch showed a guess. The page only changes the picture of the login screen.
- Start-up: there was no way to turn the splash screen off. The page has the entry "None" that the page of System Settings has (the theme `None`, which it saves with the engine `none` in `ksplashrc`, as `kcms/ksplash/kcm.cpp` of plasma-desktop 6.7.4 does), last in the list. Choosing any other theme sets the engine `KSplashQML` again.
- The scroll bar of the menu was drawn over the buttons of the pages. It has its own room now.
- The cards of the cursors had a light background; they use the dark one of the other cards now.
- Window decorations did not list the ones written in QML that KWin brings (Plastik) nor the ones installed as packages in `kwin/decorations`. They are listed now.
- Changing the icon theme did not refresh the icons of the programs that were open. System Settings throws away the cache of icons and announces the six groups of icons as changed; only the first one was announced. The six are announced now, and the cache is thrown away.
- The helper is built by `npm run build:helper`, which `tauri build` runs first. The package depends on `libqt6widgets6`, `libqt6quick6`, `libkf6svg6`, `libkf6colorscheme6`, `libkf6package6`, `libkf6configcore6`, `libkf6coreaddons6`, `libkdecorations3-6` and `libkdecorations3private2`. Building it needs `cmake`, `g++`, `qt6-base-dev`, `qt6-declarative-dev`, `libkf6svg-dev`, `libkf6colorscheme-dev`, `libkf6package-dev`, `libkf6config-dev`, `libkf6coreaddons-dev` and `libkdecorations3-dev`.
- Panel: several elements of a panel showed their identifier (`org.kde.plasma.kickoff`) and not their name. They are plugins that are compiled and have no `metadata.json` to read. The helper lists the widgets as libplasma does (`listAppletMetaData`: the packages of QML and the plugins in `plasma/applets/`), with the names in the language of the program, and the list does not offer the desktop or the folder view, which are containments.
- Panel: Apply is on the row of the panels, at the top, and that row stays where it is while the settings scroll under it (it is outside of what scrolls, so it does not depend on the engine of the window). The dialog to add an element has no icon of information, which did nothing.
- Window decorations listed `V2`, which is not a decoration but the Aurorae engine (`org.kde.kwin.aurorae.v2`), and applying it left the windows with no title bars. The plugins that list themes (both Aurorae engines) are not offered any more, as KWin does.
- A color scheme that does not say every color (the Breeze themes of Plasma leave out the background of the selection) was drawn as an icon. The colors it does not say take the ones of Breeze now, as KDE does.
- The pictures of the global themes and of the start-up screens were cut at the top and at the bottom, so the panels, the dock and the bar of the theme did not show. The card has the shape of the picture (16:9) now and shows it whole.
- The names of the global themes were cut: the picture of a theme was taller than its box and covered half of the name. The picture is cut to its box now.
- Wallpapers: a folder of loose pictures, like the 24 of Soplos, was one card with one picture, and its picture did not show because it weighed more than 2 MB. Every picture is its own card now, and the pictures are read from the disk with no limit of size. The wallpaper in use is marked.

- The icons of the icon themes showed as a blue square with a question mark when the file was a link to another file (`folder.svg` to `default-folder.svg` in Tela, the terminal and the browser in Breeze), because the protocol that shows the files to the window does not follow the relative links. The real path of the file is passed now.
- Scrolling the wallpapers stuck: every card decoded a picture of 2 to 3 MB. The cards show a small copy of the wallpaper (400 pixels, made once with `gdk-pixbuf` and kept in `~/.cache/soplos-look-manager/thumbs`), and show it as soon as it is ready.
- Choosing the Adwaita cursors failed with `Icon theme "AdwaitaLegacy" not found`, because Adwaita inherits from a theme that is not installed. A cursor theme whose parent is not installed is not offered any more.
- GTK: the theme is set as Plasma sets it, through the service `org.kde.GtkConfig` of kde-gtk-config, which keeps the files of GTK 3 and GTK 4 itself; the files are written by the program only when that service does not answer. The themes that only have key bindings (Default, Emacs) are not offered. A theme whose colors can be read is drawn as a small window with them; one whose colors cannot be read shows the picture it brings (`thumbnail.png`), whole and not cut. Adwaita, Adwaita-dark and HighContrast, whose stylesheet says nothing or only imports the one that GTK has inside, show their usual colors.
- The message of an element that cannot be applied does not say anything about Plasma running any more.

### Added
- The sidebar groups can be folded and unfolded (the state is remembered); the group of the open page stays showing it.
- Windows group: Virtual desktops (add, rename and remove desktops, rows, cyclic navigation, through the `org.kde.KWin` D-Bus service), Desktop effects and KWin scripts (a switch each, through `org.kde.kwin.Effects`, `org.kde.kwin.Scripting` and `kwinrc`) and Task switcher (the layout of `[TabBox]` in `kwinrc`). The lists come from the helper, which reads the packages of KWin.
- Windows group: Behavior (the options of Window Behavior of System Settings: focus policy and focus stealing prevention, raising, placement of new windows, snapping, and what the mouse does on the title bar and inside the windows; the keys and stored values are the ones of `kcms/options` in KWin 6.7, written to `kwinrc` and applied with `reloadConfig`) and Rules (the window rules of `kwinrulesrc`: add, edit, enable and remove a rule that matches a window by class and title, with the properties position, size, maximized, minimized, full screen, above, below, no border, skip taskbar, pager and switcher, screen and opacity, each with its action: force, apply initially, remember, apply now or force temporarily).
- Remove from the pages: a trash button on the card (or the row) of what the user installed, after a confirmation. Icon and cursor themes of the system (`/usr/share/icons`) can be removed too, through the same pkexec step as the login screen (`RemoveSystemIcons`), which asks for the administrator password; the fallback theme `hicolor` and the one in use cannot. A system package that is removed this way comes back if its package is installed again.
- Install from the pages: Get new... opens the store window of KDE (`knewstuff-dialog6` with the `.knsrc` of the kind) and Install from file... installs a package (`kpackagetool6`), unpacks an icon, cursor, sound, GTK or decoration archive, copies a color scheme or a wallpaper. New dependencies: `kpackagetool6`, `knewstuff-dialog6`, `kdialog | zenity`, `unzip`.
- The pages of the Global theme of System Settings that were missing: Colors (the color schemes, drawn with their colors and applied with `plasma-apply-colorscheme`), Application style (Breeze, Fusion, Windows and the other styles of Qt that are installed), Plasma style (the themes of the panel and the widgets, applied with `plasma-apply-desktoptheme`), Window decorations (the plugins of KWin and the themes of Aurorae, with a reconfigure of KWin) and Sounds (the sound themes). The menu is in three groups (Look, Desktop and Start-up), it scrolls when the window is short, and the shortcuts Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 are for the first nine pages.
- Panel: Add panel opens a menu with the three templates of Plasma (the default panel, an empty panel and the bar of the menu of the application), where it always added a panel with a launcher, the tasks, the tray and the clock. The panel has the options of the panel preferences of Plasma: alignment, width (fill, fit the content, custom, with the minimum and the maximum length), offset, opacity (adaptive, opaque, translucent), as well as the position, the height, floating and hiding it had. The numbers (offset and lengths) are changed with – and + buttons, as in Theme Manager, and not with the small arrows that the toolkit draws. The page is laid out around a drawing of the screen with the panel where and as the settings say (position, alignment, width, thickness, floating and opacity), which is redrawn as they change and where a click on an edge of the screen moves the panel; the options are in three cards (position and size, look, behavior), and three styles set several things at once: a classic panel, a dock (floating, fit to the content, in the middle) and a side bar. Removing a panel is the icon of the panel in view. Each property is read and written on its own, so one that this version of Plasma does not have does not stop the others.
- Applying a global theme asks first what to apply, as System Settings does: the appearance settings (on) and the layout of the desktop and the windows (off, because it replaces the panels and the widgets). The layout is applied with `plasma-apply-lookandfeel --resetLayout`, or alone through the shell of Plasma.
- Icons show six icons of each theme (a folder, the home, a terminal, the settings, a browser and a text editor), and Cursors show four cursors, read from the format of X and kept in `~/.cache/soplos-look-manager`.
- GTK shows a small window painted with the colors of each theme, read from its stylesheets; a theme that does not say its colors as plain colors keeps the icon.

## [3.0.0-alpha] - 2026-11-08

First version, for testing on Soplos Linux Tyson (Plasma). It has not yet been run on a Plasma desktop: report anything that fails.

### Added
- Soplos Look Manager 3.0 on Tauri 2, Svelte 4 and Tailwind 3: the theme and layout manager of Soplos Linux Tyson. It is a new program, with no 2.x version to port.
- Frameless card window with the sidebar, light and dark theme that follows the desktop, and the interface in 8 languages (en, es, de, fr, it, pt, ro, ru). Every page has a banner with the image of the program.
- Keyboard shortcuts: F1 for About, Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8 for the pages, Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab for the next and the previous page, and Ctrl+Q to quit. The right button of the mouse is off in the installed program.
- Themes: lists the global themes of Plasma, with a preview, and applies one with `plasma-apply-lookandfeel`.
- Wallpapers: lists the wallpapers of the system and of the user, with a preview, and applies one with `plasma-apply-wallpaperimage`.
- Icons and Cursors: list the installed themes and apply them (`plasma-changeicons`, `plasma-apply-cursortheme`).
- GTK: lists the GTK themes, and sets the one chosen for GTK 3, GTK 4 and the settings of the desktop, linking the files of the theme into the folder of GTK 4 for libadwaita. A file of the user is never overwritten.
- Panel: lists the panels of Plasma and changes the one chosen (position, height, floating and when it hides). A panel can be added, with the launcher, the tasks, the system tray and the clock, and removed, never the last one.
- Start-up: lists the start-up screens and sets the one chosen.
- Login: sets the picture of the login screen of Plasma Login Manager, from the wallpapers installed, and whether NumLock is on at login. It asks for the administrator password through polkit (`org.soplos.lookmanager.run-privileged`); the program checks everything again as root, and only a picture of the wallpapers of the system or of the user who asked can be used.
- The package: launcher in 8 languages (only shown in Plasma), AppStream metadata, copyright file and the polkit action.
